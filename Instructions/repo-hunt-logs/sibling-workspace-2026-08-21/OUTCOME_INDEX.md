## Interpret the labels carefully

- **Accepted** means a user-confirmed platform outcome when the manifest says
  so. It is not inferred from local tests or a solve-rate forecast.
- **Rejected/closed** can mean the task identity failed even when the repository
  remains eligible for a materially different subsystem.
- **Calibration failure** means a valid problem missed the difficulty or
  long-horizon band; it does not mean the implementation was incorrect.
- **Environment failure** is never a failed solver solution.
- **Historical** records can remain useful evidence without being current
  policy precedent.
