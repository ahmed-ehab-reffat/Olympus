## Cross-cutting lesson

**A friend's proven-repo list is a derivative-risk list, not a lead list.** Stage 0-bis is right that
proven repos beat cold starts — but its evidence is repos WE mined, where we can see every lane we
took. A repo another author is mining gives us the paid-for mechanical measurements and none of the
lane visibility, and the dedup engine compares behavioural overlap across all authors. Before
following a referral into a repo, ask WHICH LANES the referrer took; without that the referral moves
derivative risk in the wrong direction.

## ⛔ ADDENDUM 2026-09-08 (same day) — ytt RANK 1 was WRONG; the repo is DEAD

A full Phase-3 pass followed this hunt and reversed its top result. 8 candidates, 6 rejected at
screening, 2 carried to trap reproduction and both killed. Zero shippable. Details in
`SATURATED-REPOS.md § B2-YTT`, artifacts in `rejected/ytt-overlay-write-provenance/` and

**Why the ranking was wrong, and the correction to make in the NEXT hunt.** This log ranked ytt first
on mechanical cleanliness plus seam richness: Apache-2.0, pure Go, vendored deps, green deterministic
baseline, zero subs against quota, and one overlay kernel with four call sites and three calling
conventions carrying F-2/F-6/F-7/F-9. Every one of those held up under scrutiny. **None of them
predicted authorability.** Both reproduced deaths were invisible to all ten PICK-FILTER gates and to
the entire F-catalogue:

- overlay write-provenance -> bookkeeping, difficulty collapses to one storage-location insight;
- library schema-override compatibility -> the convergent architecture is **~25 effective LOC**
  reusing existing primitives, despite a `grep` proving no Type-vs-Type comparison existed.

**Two ranking inputs to add before the next sweep runs:**
1. **Absorption is REACHABILITY, not naming.** Spend ten minutes trying to solve the candidate with
   the repo's existing primitives BEFORE sketching a footprint table. A clean grep is not clearance.
2. **Score the repo's SHAPE, not just its hygiene.** Generic, complete internals (a tree engine, a
   type tree, a visitor, a matcher algebra) absorb everything layered on them; when the remainder is
   fixed by an external standard, there is no authorable middle. Prefer a real DOMAIN over a mature
   framework.

The mechanical work here is still worth keeping — lyon and thermo's verdicts stand unchanged.
