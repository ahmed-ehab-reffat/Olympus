## Workshop (1 spike sub-agent, finished before return) — asdf composite
Spike brief: build lane 1 (lazy_tree pass-through write, measured 176 in hunt #31) plus #1795
(info/search without conversion) on ONE kernel, then measure and guard it.

**Result: PASS, measured 362 human-eff.**
- Totals: padding-floor 296, raw 438, 6 files: `_pass_through.py` new 252, `search.py` 43,
  `_node_info.py` 38, `_asdf.py` 19, `yamlutil.py` 6, `_display.py` 4.
- The #1795 half alone is 195 (floor 175). Core is ~240: lane 1 ~140, plus the LazyView decision
  logic ~80, plus the two-phase filters ~20.
- I re-ran the hook myself and got 362. The patch uses 14 docstring/comment lines; the repo does
  docstring its source.
- Full suite with the prototype: 2194 passed / 2 xfailed, same as the clean baseline.

**The kernel.**
- The shared pieces are `_raw_items`, a cache-or-raw lookup in `_tagged_object_cache`, and
  `materialize()`, which converts through the lazy container so the result is cached AND stored in
  the tree.
- The writer's `_resolve` and the new `LazyView` both use it. `LazyView` drives info, search (paths,
  nodes, repr, chained search) and schema_info.
- When a node gets converted:
  - when descent requires it: the node's single declared type is a container or has
    `__asdf_traverse__`, or (info only) its converter has `to_info`;
  - when its converter declares more than one type;
  - when a `value=`/`filter_` predicate must be evaluated, and only after every `key`/`type_`
    criterion across the whole search chain has accepted the node;
  - when `.node`/`.nodes` returns it.
- ndarray info (shape/dtype) comes from the raw node, and `type_` comes from the declared type.

**F2P probes** (`probe_ci_1..8` plus lane 1's `probe_pt_1..7`, all in `s_0926h33/asdf_composite/`).
I re-ran P1 and P5 myself against combined, lane-1-only and base:
- P1: base and lane-1-only raise `ValueError` from a broken converter on `info()` and on
  `search(key=)`. The combined prototype renders the tree with 0 converter calls.
- P5: mutate one node through a search result, then `write_to`. Combined makes 1 to-call and keeps
  3 tags at 1.0.0. Lane-1-only and base make 3 to-calls and re-tag at 1.1.0.
- P4/P5/P7 fail with lane 1 alone, so the two halves are not separable.
- P6: the full `info()` text is md5-identical to base, with 0 array conversions (base makes 5).

**Stage 6 death-class guard (run by me on the spike's evidence):**
1. Shared kernel: YES. Mutant M1 converts outside the tree. It passes every info/search probe and
   silently loses the mutation in write-after-search (P5/P7). A local fix on one surface regresses
   another, and this was measured.
2. Interdependent + misdirecting: YES.
   - T4 (materialize through the tree) is coupled to the writer, and its failure surfaces in the
     written file, not in search.
   - T2 (a raw TaggedDict is a dict; alias recursion) is coupled to T4 through cache resolution. The
     M4 mutant drops `root['y']`.
   - T3 (two-phase filters across a chain): the M3 mutant raises on P2.
   - T5 (ndarray info from raw) and T7 (`type_` from the declared type) share one rule.
   - Lane 1's T3-T7 are carried over.
3. Standalone file / post-pass: NO post-pass is possible, because the conversion decision is made
   inside three existing walkers (writer, info tree builder, search BFS). Still, 252 of 362 lines sit
   in a new module, wired in at ~12 sites across 5 files. Recorded as a RISK, not a kill: the module
   is the kernel the existing subsystems call, not a pass over their output.
4. TOO-EASY Pre-Pick Guard:
   - #1: no uniform wrap; the rules differ by node kind.
   - #2: multi-subsystem (lazy_nodes cache, yamlutil writer, block manager, _node_info, search,
     _display).
   - #3: RISK. The contract needs ~8-10 sentences: the descend/convert rule, multi-type converters,
     chain-wide filter order, hidden leaf value, declared-type `type_`, and lane 1's private-block,
     version and cross-file rules.
   - #4: not a port. libasdf (C) reads raw YAML but has no converters, and no sibling Python ASDF
     library exists.
   - #5: T2/T3/T4 survive full specification; T5/T7 weaken once stated.

VALUE, not cost: the discriminators are behavioural (a raising converter, tag versions kept, a
mutation written, a warning count, identical info text), not call counts.
