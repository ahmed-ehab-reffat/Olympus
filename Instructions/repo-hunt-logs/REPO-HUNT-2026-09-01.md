**The trap worth naming for next time.** I twice talked myself past PR #135 — first "different core

art looks adjacent, do not argue about file overlap — find the load-bearing operation and check
whether the prior art describes it.** File-set reasoning is the cheap test; the load-bearing-operation
test is the correct one.
