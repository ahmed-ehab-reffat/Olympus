# Add a cost-basis and tax-lot accounting ledger to techan

Add a `costbasis` package tracking realized and unrealized P&L across orders. `NewLedger(method Method, longTerm time.Duration) *Ledger` creates a ledger whose `Apply(order techan.Order, commission big.Decimal)` records a trade: a same-side or flat trade opens a lot; an opposing trade closes lots; excess flips, opening a lot at the trade price. Closing a long realizes `(price - lotPrice) * quantity` and closing a short realizes `(lotPrice - price) * quantity`.

`Method` selects which lot a close matches: `FIFO` (oldest), `LIFO` (newest), `HIFO` (highest cost), `LOFO` (lowest cost), or `Average`, which merges same-side trades into one lot whose price is `(existingAmount*existingPrice + tradeAmount*tradePrice) / (existingAmount + tradeAmount)`. Cost matching uses each lot's current, wash-adjusted price. `CloseLot(order techan.Order, lot int, commission big.Decimal) bool` closes a caller-chosen lot index, returning false when the side does not oppose, the index is invalid, or the amount exceeds it.

Commissions are tracked separately; `RealizedPnL` is `GrossRealizedPnL` minus `Commissions`. A close is long-term (`LongTermRealized`) when held strictly longer than `longTerm`, else short-term (`ShortTermRealized`). Queries are `Position` (signed net quantity), `CostBasis` (total open cost), `AverageCost` (`CostBasis` divided by absolute position, or zero when flat), and `UnrealizedPnL(mark)`. `OpenLots` returns mutable copies of the open `Lot` values (`Amount`, `Price`, `Acquired`) in creation order, and `Events` a `RealizedEvent` (fields `PnL` and `OpenPrice`, the matched lot's wash-adjusted basis) per lot matched by a close, in match order. Statistics count each matched-lot event: `WinCount`, `LossCount`, `BreakevenCount`, `GrossProfit`, `GrossLoss`, `LargestWin`, `LargestLoss` (`GrossLoss` and `LargestLoss` are non-positive). `TaxSummary` (a struct with `Proceeds`, `CostBasisClosed`, and `NetRealized`; `Proceeds` sums each close at its trade price, `CostBasisClosed` sums matched lots' basis, and `NetRealized` is net of commissions and disallowed losses).

`NewWashSaleLedger(method, longTerm, washWindow)` additionally applies the wash-sale rule: when a purchase falls within `washWindow` (inclusive) after a loss closing a long, unwashed losses, most recent first, are disallowed up to the purchased quantity, removed from realized results (not the statistics), then reported by the `Disallowed` query as a positive magnitude, added to the new lot's basis across the purchased shares, and the acquisition date tacks to it. Losses wash share by share: each share is disallowed at most once, and unmatched shares stay eligible for later purchases. Only shares opening or extending a long lot wash; a buy merely covering a short does not.

Corporate actions adjust lots: `Split(ratio big.Decimal)` multiplies each amount and divides each price by ratio (a non-positive ratio is ignored); `ReturnOfCapital(perShare big.Decimal)` lowers each lot's price by perShare, floored at zero; `DividendReinvest(perShare, price big.Decimal, t time.Time)` buys `position*perShare/price` shares at price; does nothing unless the position is long. `NewPortfolio(method Method, longTerm time.Duration) *Portfolio`'s `Apply(order, commission)` routes each order by `order.Security`. `Securities() []string` and `Positions() map[string]big.Decimal` cover every traded security, including ones now flat (zero quantity); `RealizedPnL`, `UnrealizedPnL`, `NetLiquidationValue` (realized plus unrealized), and `TaxSummary` combine per-security ledgers, mark-based methods taking `map[string]big.Decimal` marks; unmarked securities contribute nothing. `LongExposure` and `ShortExposure` are positive marked values of net-long and net-short securities, `GrossExposure` their sum and `NetExposure` `LongExposure` minus `ShortExposure`.
