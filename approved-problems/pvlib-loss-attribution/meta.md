# Add loss attribution to the ModelChain simulation pipeline

A ModelChain reports AC power but not where the rest of the sunlight went. Add `ModelChain.run_loss_attribution(weather)`, `ModelChain.run_loss_attribution_from_poa(data)` and `ModelChain.run_loss_attribution_from_effective_irradiance(data)`. Each runs the ordinary simulation for that starting point, decomposes it, returns the chain, and stores a `LossAttribution` on `results.loss_attribution`. The ordinary run_model and its two variants leave that attribute unset.

The buckets follow from replaying the model through a fixed stage order. The reference stage uses clear-sky irradiance on a horizontal surface, no reflection or spectral loss, a cell temperature of 25 degrees Celsius, no DC ohmic or other DC loss, and takes AC power to be DC power summed over the arrays. Each later stage switches one mechanism to its modeled value, keeping everything earlier stages switched on, in this order: `transposition`, `weather`, `reflection`, `spectral`, `temperature`, `dc_ohmic`, `dc_other`, `inverter`. A bucket is the drop its own stage caused, so the reference less the eight buckets equals the AC power the run produced, reported as `ac`. A bucket is negative when its mechanism raises power.

Clear-sky irradiance comes from the chain's own clear-sky, airmass and transposition models, at the same solar position as the rest of the run. Irradiance without reflection, or without spectral loss, is not plane-of-array global irradiance: it is the chain's own effective irradiance with that modifier replaced by one.

`reference` and the first seven buckets are per array, in the shape ModelChain already uses: a bare Series for one array, a tuple for several, and a one-element tuple when a single-array system is run with list- or tuple-wrapped input. `inverter` and `ac` are system wide, and `num_arrays` counts the arrays.

Starting from effective irradiance leaves the first four buckets undefined, so they are zero and the reference uses the supplied effective irradiance.

The eight buckets depend on stage order. `shapley()` removes that dependence for the six conversion mechanisms `reflection`, `spectral`, `temperature`, `dc_ohmic`, `dc_other` and `inverter`, holding orientation and sky at their modeled values: each is credited the mean drop it causes over all orderings of those six, and a combination with `inverter` switched off again takes AC power to be DC summed over the arrays. It returns a dictionary keyed by them of system-wide Series summing elementwise to the `weather` column of `cumulative()` less `ac`, and computing it leaves `results` and the eight sequential buckets as they were.

`to_frame()` returns a DataFrame on the model index with columns `reference`, those bucket names in the same order, then `ac`, summing per-array buckets over the arrays. `to_frame(per_array=True)` gives a MultiIndex of array position over those names, splitting inverter loss in proportion to the DC each array delivered, equally where none delivered any, so summing over arrays reproduces the system frame. `cumulative()` returns a DataFrame of the power remaining after each stage, with columns `reference` then the eight bucket names. `energies()` returns a Series of those columns totalled over the index and indexed by them, over the per-array columns when asked for per array, and a DataFrame of one row per period when a resampling frequency is given. `as_fraction()` gives each bucket's energy as a fraction of reference energy, keyed by the eight bucket names, zero whenever that reference energy is zero, and a tuple of one such dictionary per array when asked for per array.
