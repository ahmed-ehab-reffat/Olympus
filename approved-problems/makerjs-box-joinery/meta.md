# Add finger joint generation for laser cut box faces

Maker.js cannot cut finger joints. Add a `joinery` namespace and the models `JointedRectangle` and `FingerBox`. `joinery.fingerCount(length, fingerWidth)` divides an edge into an odd number of equal fingers, never fewer than three: the odd count nearest `length / fingerWidth`, the larger when two tie. `joinery.fingers(length, options)` returns those spans in order as `{start, end, tab}`, alternating, starting with a tab unless `startWithTab` is false. Options carry `fingerWidth`, `thickness`, `kerf` and `startWithTab`. A kerf moves every interior boundary half a kerf toward the neighbouring slot; the outer boundaries stay put.

`joinery.complement(fingers, kerf)` returns the mating partition; applying it twice returns the original. `joinery.mates(a, b, kerf)` reports whether two partitions divide the edge the same way with opposite polarity once the kerf comes off. `joinery.profile(fingers, thickness)` traces the edge along x from start to end as an array of `[x, y]` points, two per finger, tabs at y zero, slots at y equal to the thickness.

`joinery.mortises(fingers, thickness, kerf)` returns a model whose child models, `Mortise1` upward, each close a pocket over one tab, running along y and centred on x. A pocket spans `thickness - kerf` across x and two kerfs less than the widened tab along y. `joinery.report(model)` counts, over a model and its children, those carrying `joints` as `faces`, their `tabs` and `slots`, and total path length as `cutLength`.

`JointedRectangle(width, height, options)` also reads `edges`, mapping any of `bottom`, `right`, `top` and `left` to that edge's polarity; absent edges stay straight. Walk the outline counterclockwise from the origin, sink slots inward, name the lines `Edge1` upward, expose the fingers as `joints`. A slot at a corner swallows it; where both edges are slotted the outline turns at the point set in by the thickness from each.

`FingerBox(width, depth, height, options)` builds `front` and `back` at width by height, `left` and `right` at depth by height, `bottom` and `top` at width by depth. Front and back start every edge with a tab, top and bottom with a slot, each side with a slot on its vertical edges and a tab on its horizontal ones. `seams` lists the twelve shared edges, each with a `tabbed` and a `slotted` `[face, edge]` array. `open` drops the top face and the joints meeting it. `dividers` gives distances from the left face; each stands a panel of depth by height, named `divider1` onward, edges all slot-leading, and puts its pockets into `bottom`, `top`, `front` and `back` as a child model named after the panel.

Every function and model here rejects a bad argument with a message containing `joinery:`: any length, width, depth, height, finger width or thickness not above zero, an empty finger list given to `complement`, `profile`, `mates` or `mortises`, a negative kerf, a kerf reaching the width of a finger being divided or the thickness given to `mortises`, and a divider not strictly between zero and the width. Kerf arguments are optional, defaulting to zero.
