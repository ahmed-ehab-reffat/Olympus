# Add temperature dependent thermochemistry to chempy

Add a `chempy.thermochemistry` subpackage for temperature dependent heat capacity models and the enthalpy and entropy that follow from them. Use SI units throughout: J/mol, J/(mol K), kelvin and pascal. Expose `molar_gas_constant` 8.314462618, `reference_temperature` 298.15 and `standard_pressure` 1e5 Pa. The formulas below abbreviate the gas constant as R.

Three models answer `heat_capacity(T)`, `enthalpy(T)` and `entropy(T)` and expose their range as `T_min` and `T_max`, raising `ValueError` outside it though the limits stay valid. Enthalpy is relative to 298.15 K, entropy is absolute. `Shomate` takes coefficients A to H, `T_min` and `T_max`, and with t=T/1000 answers `A+B*t+C*t**2+D*t**3+E/t**2`, `1000*(A*t+B*t**2/2+C*t**3/3+D*t**4/4-E/t+F-H)` and `A*log(t)+B*t+C*t**2/2+D*t**3/3-E/(2*t**2)+G`. `NASA7` takes the seven coefficients a1 to a7 as one sequence, `T_min` and `T_max`, rejects any other count, and answers `R*(a1+a2*T+a3*T**2+a4*T**3+a5*T**4)`, `R*T*(a1+a2*T/2+a3*T**2/3+a4*T**3/4+a5*T**4/5+a6/T)` less its own value at 298.15 K, and `R*(a1*log(T)+a2*T+a3*T**2/2+a4*T**3/3+a5*T**4/4+a7)`.

`PiecewiseThermo` takes segments as given, in the order given, and does not require them to be sorted, contiguous or disjoint; it spans all their ranges and answers from the first whose range contains the temperature, so a shared boundary belongs to the earlier one; `segment_at(T)` returns that segment, and `discontinuities()` gives a `(temperature, heat capacity, enthalpy, entropy)` tuple, each jump later minus earlier, at every boundary two consecutive segments share, meaning the earlier one's `T_max` equals the later one's `T_min`.

A multi segment model is reconciled by shifting each segment by the jumps accumulated along the chain of shared boundaries linking it to the segment holding 298.15 K, or to the lowest segment if none does, which stays unshifted; a gap or an overlap breaks that chain, so a segment the chain never reaches keeps its own values. `enthalpy(T)` and `entropy(T)` are continuous at every boundary the chain crosses. `heat_capacity(T)` is never shifted and `discontinuities()` still reports raw jumps.

`Shomate.fit(temperatures,heat_capacities,enthalpy,entropy,T_ref,T_min,T_max)` returns a `Shomate` whose heat capacity is the least squares fit to the tabulated data and whose enthalpy and entropy at `T_ref` are the two supplied values; its range defaults to the extremes of the data, and mismatched lengths or fewer than five points are rejected. `SubstanceThermo` pairs a model with a standard enthalpy of formation defaulting to zero, adds it to the enthalpy but not the entropy, and adds `gibbs_energy(T)`.

`reaction_heat_capacity`, `reaction_enthalpy` and `reaction_entropy` weight a mapping of species to `SubstanceThermo` by the net stoichiometry of a `Reaction`, and `reaction_gibbs_energy`, `equilibrium_constant` and `as_equilibrium` follow, the last an `Equilibrium` carrying the constant and the reaction's own reactants and products. `mixture_enthalpy`, `mixture_heat_capacity` and `mixture_entropy` weight by moles instead, skipping a species with no amount before any thermochemistry is looked up, so such a species needs none. An ideal gas activity is a mole fraction times `pressure/standard_pressure`, which `reaction_quotient` and `gibbs_energy_at_composition` use, both rejecting a species of non zero net stoichiometry that is absent or at zero amount because its activity would make the quotient undefined. A species that cancels to a zero net stoichiometry takes no part in any reaction quantity, so it needs neither an amount nor thermochemistry, though an amount given for it still counts in the mole fractions the way an inert does; `entropy_of_mixing` is `-R*sum(n*log(x))` and `total_entropy` also subtracts `total*R*log(pressure/standard_pressure)`.

`adiabatic_temperature` conserves the enthalpy of the whole mixture over a turnover `extent`, one mole by default, and an `extent` of zero leaves the temperature where it started; `equilibrium_temperature` inverts the constant inside a bracket, and a root sitting exactly on either limit of that bracket counts as bracketed rather than as a failure; `equilibrium_composition` returns the amount of every species given or named by the reaction where the quotient equals the constant; `isentropic_temperature` takes the pressure before and after and conserves the total entropy; `adiabatic_equilibrium` returns a `(temperature, amounts)` tuple;

`simultaneous_equilibrium` equilibrates reactions until they converge, giving up after `max_iterations`; and `adiabatic_simultaneous_equilibrium` does what `adiabatic_equilibrium` does but for a set of reactions, re-equilibrating the whole set at every candidate temperature so the enthalpy balance is taken against a composition that moves with it, and returning a `(temperature, amounts)` tuple in which every reaction meets its own constant at the temperature found.

Keep these names and this argument order exactly: `reaction_heat_capacity(reaction,thermo,T)` and likewise `reaction_enthalpy`, `reaction_entropy`, `reaction_gibbs_energy`, `equilibrium_constant` and `as_equilibrium`; `mixture_enthalpy(amounts,thermo,T)` and likewise `mixture_heat_capacity` and `mixture_entropy`; `entropy_of_mixing(amounts)`; `total_entropy(amounts,thermo,T,pressure)`; `reaction_quotient(amounts,reaction,pressure)`; `gibbs_energy_at_composition(amounts,reaction,thermo,T,pressure)`; `adiabatic_temperature(amounts,reaction,thermo,T,extent,tolerance)`; `equilibrium_temperature(reaction,thermo,K,T_min,T_max,tolerance)`; `equilibrium_composition(amounts,reaction,thermo,T,pressure,tolerance)`; `isentropic_temperature(amounts,thermo,T,pressure,final_pressure,tolerance)`; `adiabatic_equilibrium(amounts,reaction,thermo,T,pressure,tolerance)`; `simultaneous_equilibrium(amounts,reactions,thermo,T,pressure,tolerance,max_iterations)`; and `adiabatic_simultaneous_equilibrium(amounts,reactions,thermo,T,pressure,tolerance,max_iterations)`.

A reaction species missing from `amounts` starts at zero in the searches. `equilibrium_composition`, `simultaneous_equilibrium`, `adiabatic_equilibrium` and `adiabatic_simultaneous_equilibrium` return a mapping of species to amounts, the last two inside their tuple.

`tolerance` is optional everywhere it appears and defaults to 1e-9, `max_iterations` is optional and defaults to 200, and `pressure` defaults to the standard pressure. A `tolerance` must be positive: kelvin for a temperature solve, a fraction of the feasible extent range for `equilibrium_composition`. A model whose `T_min` exceeds its `T_max` is rejected. `ValueError` covers a missing species, a non positive amount or pressure where a positive one is needed, an empty mixture or an empty sequence of segments or reactions, a temperature out of range, an unbracketed search and a sweep that never converges. The four classes import from `chempy` too.
